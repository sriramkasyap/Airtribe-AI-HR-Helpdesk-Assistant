# Code of Conduct (pol2)

**Category:** conduct  
**Effective:** 1 January 2024  
**Status:** active

## 1. Purpose

All employees must follow this code of conduct. It is the minimum standard for how we treat colleagues, candidates, customers, and company information.

## 2. Respect and inclusion

- Do not harass, discriminate, or retaliate against anyone based on a protected characteristic.
- Give feedback on work, not on the person.
- Escalate interpersonal issues to your manager or HR rather than airing them in public channels.

## 3. Confidentiality

Employee records, compensation, health information, and unpublished product plans are confidential. Do not share another employee’s data with anyone who does not have a job-related need to know — including via the HR helpdesk assistant.

## 4. Conflicts of interest

Disclose outside employment, vendor relationships, or personal relationships in the reporting line to HR. Do not approve spend or performance outcomes for someone you have a close personal relationship with.

## 5. Reporting

Report suspected violations to HR or anonymously through the ethics inbox. Good-faith reports are protected from retaliation. HR investigates and may involve Legal. Confirmed violations can lead to coaching, a written warning, or termination.
