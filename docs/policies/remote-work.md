# Remote Work Policy (pol1)

**Category:** remote_work  
**Effective:** 1 January 2024  
**Status:** active

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes when employees may work away from a company office and what is expected of them on those days.

## 2. Eligibility

Full-time employees who have completed 90 days of employment may request a hybrid remote schedule. Interns, contractors, and employees on a performance-improvement plan must work from the office unless HR grants a written exception.

## 3. Schedule

- Employees may work remotely **up to 3 days per week**.
- At least **2 days per week** must be spent in the assigned office.
- Teams pick their in-office days so collaboration is predictable. Managers publish the team’s default pattern each quarter.

## 4. Core hours and availability

On remote days, employees must be reachable on Slack and email between **10:00 and 16:00** in the office time zone, except during a scheduled lunch break. Video is required for scheduled meetings unless the invite says otherwise.

## 5. Workspace and equipment

Employees are responsible for a private, safe workspace with reliable internet. The company provides a laptop; home-office furniture and extra monitors are reimbursed only under the travel / office-supplies reimbursement process.

## 6. Exceptions

A manager and HR may approve a temporary fully-remote stretch (for example travel or a family emergency) for up to 15 calendar days. Longer arrangements need a written HR exception.
