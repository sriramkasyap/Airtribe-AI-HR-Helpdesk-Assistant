# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy (pol3)

**Category:** leave  
**Effective:** 1 January 2024  
**Status:** active

## 1. Purpose

Employees are entitled to paid time off based on tenure. This policy covers casual leave, sick leave, and earned leave. It does not cover parental leave or statutory holidays, which have separate documents.

## 2. Annual entitlements (full-time)

| Tenure              | Casual leave | Sick leave | Earned leave |
|---------------------|--------------|------------|--------------|
| 0–12 months         | 12 days      | 8 days     | 10 days      |
| 1–3 years           | 18 days      | 12 days    | 15 days      |
| 3+ years            | 21 days      | 12 days    | 18 days      |

Part-time employees receive a pro-rated entitlement. Unused **earned** leave may carry over up to 5 days into the next calendar year; casual and sick leave reset on 1 January.

## 3. How to request leave

- Casual and earned leave: submit the request at least **3 working days** in advance (5 days if the stretch is longer than 3 days).
- Sick leave: notify your manager as soon as you can on the day of absence. A medical note is required after 3 consecutive sick days.
- Managers approve or decline within 2 working days. The helpdesk can report **balances** but cannot approve leave.

## 4. What the helpdesk may disclose

An employee may see only their own balances. A manager may look up a direct or cross-team employee’s balance by employee ID or name for planning. The assistant must never invent a balance; it must read the employee record.
